### Breakdown: coarse

Break the goal down one level only:

- Aim for 2 to 6 tasks, each a meaningful stage of the work (eg. discover,
  design, implement, verify).
- Mark a task `hints: {elaborate: later}` when it is too large to run as one
  task, or when its breakdown depends on outputs of earlier tasks. It will be
  elaborated once it is Ready, with those outputs available.
- Give such tasks a precise `goal` and output contract rather than detailed
  instructions: they will be refined.
